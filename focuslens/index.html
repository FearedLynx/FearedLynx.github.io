FocusLens

Your brain took a break. Your glasses didn't. FocusLens runs on Meta's AI glasses: when your mind drifts in a lecture or a meeting, tap, and hear what you just missed and your next step. Built by my team at Junction 2026 for Meta's challenge.

The idea

Built for people with ADHD or dyslexia, and for anyone overwhelmed. When attention slips in a lecture or a meeting, the thread is usually gone, and taking out a phone to catch up makes it worse. FocusLens brings the help to the moment of distraction, without a phone in your hand.

How it works

  • Always ready, nothing kept: the phone in your pocket holds only the last couple of minutes of audio in memory.
  • One gesture: a pinch on the Meta Neural Band (or a tap) makes the glasses take one photo of what you see.
  • One AI call: photo and audio go to a small backend, where a Gemini model returns what you missed and one concrete next step.
  • Back in your view: the answer appears on the glasses' display, or is read aloud on glasses without one, and a short context card is saved for later.
